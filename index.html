Hi! I am last year a PhD student in Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at University of Trieste. By background I am a Computer Scientist and I did my master and bachelor degree at University of Modena and Reggio Emilia and since then I have been passionate about multidisciplinary problems.
I am doing my PhD in collaboration with the research institute AREA Science Park and I am advised by Matteo Biagetti. During my PhD I also did a visiting period at INRIA in the Datashape team in Sophia-Antipolis and I collaborate with Mathieu Carrière.
My researches focus on applications of Topological Data Analysis to Artificial Intelligence models and biological data. I currently looking for Postdocs in multidisciplinary settings or opportunities in companies or startups.
Looking for opportunities in 2027!

Research

My research focuses on Topological Data Analysis and Interpretability of Large Language Models. My recent works explore:

  • Intepretability of LLMs (also with TDA) (ICML'25, GTML'25, TMLR'26)
  • Topological Data Analysis on neuroscience data ( GTML'25)
  • Complex Systems (Scientific Reports'26, Sensors'24)
  • Protein Language Model (BioArXiv'26)
