Hi! I am a PhD student in Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at University of Trieste. I am doing my PhD in collaboration with the research institute AREA Science Park and I am advised by Matteo Biagetti.
At the moment, I am doing a visiting period at INRIA in the Datashape team in Sophia-Antipolis and I collaborate with Mathieu Carrière.
My researches focus on applications of Topological Data Analysis to Artificial Intelligence models and biological data. By background I am a Computer Scientist and I did my master and bachelor degree at University of Modena and Reggio Emilia and since then I have been passionate about multidisciplinary problems

Research

My research focuses on Topological Data Analysis and Interpretability of Large Language Models. My recent works explore:

  • Intepretability with Topological Data Analysis (ICML'25, ArXiv'25)
  • Topological Data Analysis on neuroscience data (soon)
  • Complex Systems (ArXiv'25, Sensors'24)
